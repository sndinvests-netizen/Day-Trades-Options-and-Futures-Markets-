Gamma exposure (GEX)

Loading gamma exposure…
Calls / positive GEX Puts / negative GEX ━ Stock price ╍ Call wall ╍ Put wall ┅ Gamma flip Right-click a wall or strike to draw it on your TradingView chart
Yahoo data, ~15 min delayed; open interest is as of the prior close; dealer positioning assumed (dealers long the calls and short the puts customers trade, so call GEX counts positive and put GEX negative). GEX is dollars of stock dealers would buy or sell per 1% move: gamma × open interest × 100 × price² × 1%. Positive GEX: dealers hedge against the move, which tends to dampen swings. Negative GEX: they hedge with it, which tends to amplify them. When Yahoo's open interest is blank (overnight and early morning) it comes from CBOE's delayed quotes instead.

Stocks at support

Open the Screener to scan.

Support = the nearest level at or below the last close among swing lows from the last 12 months of daily lows (a low that is the lowest of the 5 bars on each side; swing lows within 1.5% are one level, and touches counts them) and the 200- and 50-day moving averages. At support = the close is 0% to the distance you set above that level, and no close in the last 5 sessions was more than 2% below it.     Partial close

    Close some contracts and keep the rest open. Short legs are bought back, long legs are sold. Leave a leg at 0 to keep all of it. Price per share is prefilled at the current mid.

    Add to position

    Buy more of this contract (for example a LEAPS on a dip). The new contracts are merged into the position at the weighted average price, so P&L and breakeven use your average cost. Price per share is prefilled at the current mid.

    Close position

    Exit price per share for each leg, prefilled at the current mid. An option at 0 is recorded as expired.
