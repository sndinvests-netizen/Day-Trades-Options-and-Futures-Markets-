## SECTION 6: WINNER AND LOSER MANAGEMENT

**On winning trades:**
- At Target 1 (1:1 R/R) → take 50% off, move stop to breakeven
- Let remainder run to Target 2 with trailing stop
- Do NOT let a winner turn into a loser without a stop in place

**On losing trades:**
- At 50% of daily max loss → reduce position size by 50%
- At 100% of daily max loss → **STOP. Close platform. Log session.**

**Ripster Clouds check (where applicable):**
- Are all clouds green and price above all? → Strong long alignment
- Are all clouds red and price below all? → Strong short alignment
