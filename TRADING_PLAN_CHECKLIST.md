# Daily Trading Plan Checklist
## Futures and Options Day Trading — Rule-Based Framework

**Trading Window: 9:30 AM – 11:00 AM ET (90 minutes ONLY)**
**No trades outside this window. No exceptions.**

## SECTION 3: ACTIVE TRADING SESSION — RULES AND DISCIPLINE (9:30 AM – 11:00 AM ET)

### 3.1 Non-Negotiable Session Rules

- [ ] I will ONLY trade between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM ET
- [ ] I will ONLY trade setups that align with the daily bias (accumulation = long, distribution = short)
- [ ] I will NOT chase price — I will wait for pullbacks and retests
- [ ] I will NOT move my stop loss against my position once a trade is live
- [ ] I will NOT add to a losing position (no averaging down)
- [ ] I will NOT trade based on emotion, FOMO, or revenge after a loss
- [ ] I will honor all pre-defined stop losses and targets without hesitation

### 3.2 Timeframe Discipline Hierarchy

Before entering any trade, confirm each timeframe:
- [ ] 15-Minute Chart: What is the macro structure and trend? (Direction and location)
- [ ] 5-Minute Chart: Is there a valid setup forming? (Setup confirmation)
- [ ] 2-Minute Chart: Is there a precise entry trigger? (Execution timing)

### 3.3 Emotional State Check (Run This Before Each Trade)

Rate your current emotional state (1 = calm and focused, 10 = anxious/frustrated):
- [ ] Current state rating: _____
- [ ] If rated 7 or higher: STOP — do not trade until you reset
- [ ] Are you trying to "make back" a loss? (If yes — STOP, this is revenge trading)
- [ ] Are you feeling FOMO from a missed move? (If yes — WAIT for the next clean setup)
- [ ] Are you second-guessing a valid setup? (If yes — review rules and execute or stand aside)

### 3.4 Maximum Trade Limits Per Session

- [ ] Maximum trades per session: _______ (define your personal limit, e.g., 3)
- [ ] Have I hit my max trade count today? (If yes — no more trades, session is over)
- [ ] Have I hit my daily max loss limit? (If yes — no more trades, log off platform)
- [ ] Have I hit my daily profit target? (Optional: lock in gains and stop trading)

## SECTION 4: TRADE SETUP EVALUATION CRITERIA

### 4.1 Pre-Entry Checklist (Complete ALL before entering any trade)

**Rule Compliance:**
- [ ] Are we within the 90-minute trading window? (If NO — DO NOT TRADE)
- [ ] Does this setup align with the daily bias? (If NO — requires 3+ strong confluence reasons)
- [ ] Have all key levels been marked on the chart? (If NO — mark them first)

**Setup Quality:**
- [ ] Is there a clear break of a key level (Opening Range, PDH, PDL, VWAP, swing level)?
- [ ] Has price pulled back and retested the broken level (ORBT pattern)?
- [ ] Is the retest holding and showing rejection or absorption?
- [ ] Is this a high-probability level (at least 2 key levels converging)?

**Indicator Confluence:**
- [ ] VWAP: Is price on the correct side of VWAP for this trade direction?
- [ ] 9 EMA: Is price respecting or bouncing from the 9 EMA in the trade direction?
- [ ] 20 MA: Does the 20 MA provide additional confluence support/resistance?
- [ ] MA Stack: Does the MA order (9 EMA vs 20 MA vs 50 MA) align with trade direction?

**Timeframe Alignment:**
- [ ] 15-Minute confirms the direction and location
- [ ] 5-Minute confirms the setup is forming
- [ ] 2-Minute confirms the entry trigger (e.g., candle close, reclaim, rejection)

## SECTION 5: RISK MANAGEMENT RULES

### 5.1 Pre-Session Risk Parameters (Define Before Market Opens)

- [ ] Account size: $_______________
- [ ] Maximum risk per trade (% of account): ______% = $_______________
- [ ] Daily maximum loss limit (% of account): ______% = $_______________
- [ ] Daily profit target (optional lock-in level): $_______________
- [ ] Maximum number of trades today: _______

### 5.2 Position Sizing Rules

- [ ] Have I calculated position size based on the distance from entry to stop loss?
- [ ] Formula: Position Size = (Max Risk $ per Trade) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)
- [ ] Does the position size keep my loss at or below my per-trade risk limit?
- [ ] Am I trading at minimum 1:2 R/R? (Target must be at least 2x the distance of my stop)

### 5.3 Stop Loss Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- [ ] Stop loss is placed below (long) or above (short) a specific structural level
- [ ] Stop loss is NOT an arbitrary round number — it is anchored to structure
- [ ] Stop loss is entered in the platform BEFORE or AT the time of entry
- [ ] I will NOT move the stop loss to a worse level once the trade is live
- [ ] I WILL move the stop loss to breakeven once the trade reaches 1:1 R/R (optional: trail stop)

### 5.4 Daily Loss Limit Protocol

- [ ] If I hit 50% of my daily max loss: Reduce position size by 50% for remaining trades
- [ ] If I hit 100% of my daily max loss: STOP TRADING. Close platform. Log the session.
- [ ] No exceptions. Capital preservation is the #1 priority.

### 5.5 Winning Trade Management

- [ ] At Target 1 (1:1 R/R): Consider taking partial profits (50% of position)
- [ ] Move stop to breakeven after partial exit
- [ ] Let remainder run toward Target 2 with a trailing stop
- [ ] Do NOT let a winning trade turn into a losing trade without a stop in place
